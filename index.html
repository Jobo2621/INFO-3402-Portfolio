About My Project

I used Power BI to look at sales data and make charts for different business questions. The data includes products, customers, stores and sales.

1. Star Schema

The factSales table has all the sales transactions. dimProduct has product info, dimCustomer has customer info, and dimStore has store info. dimDate is used for looking at sales over time. These tables connect together so the data can be analyzed easier.

2. Top Selling Products by Volume

This chart shows which products sold the most in each category.

3. Top Products by Revenue

This shows which products made the most money from sales.

4. Top Stores by Revenue

The online store makes alot more sales revenue than the other stores.

5. Most Profitable Categories

Computers made the most profit, with home appliances and cell phones next.

6. Repeat Customers

This table shows customers who ordered more than once and what products they bought.

7. Online Delivery Times

Most online deliveries took around 3 days. Some months were a little faster than others.

8. Monthly Sales and Profit

This chart shows how sales and profit changed each month between 2021 and 2024.

9. Sales by Age Group

This shows how much different age groups spend and what categories they buy. The 65+ group spent the most overall.

10. Sales by Gender

This chart looks at how much males and females spend and where they live.
